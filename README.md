# [Quentin DISSARD]

I am a graphic design student currently pursuing an MFA in Graphic and Type Design at École Estienne ([e162.eu](https://e162.eu)), after completing a BFA in Augmented Graphics and a high school diploma in STD2A (Sciences and Technologies of Design and Applied Arts).  
My work focuses on the sign, the letter, and the layout of text—whether in print or digital formats. I work freelance on various visual and editorial design projects, and I explore the web as a creative medium. You can contact me for any kind of coding and graphic design project
